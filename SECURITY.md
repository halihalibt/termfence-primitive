# Security and scope

This is a bounded semantic compression gate, not a proof of factual truth. The caller chooses the COMPLETE declared source. Missing context, false claims or deliberately omitted source units remain the caller's responsibility. All source units and candidate text are public; do not submit secrets.

Every unit is mandatory: the primitive is intentionally stricter than ordinary summarization, where omitting secondary facts may be desirable. Use short, self-contained, mutually consistent factual units. It does not discover atomic facts, resolve a contradictory source, or establish legal sufficiency. English operational prose is the demonstrated scope; other languages are not benchmarked.

The model interprets actor, modality, negation, conditions, numbers and exceptions. Independent validators must agree on the entire decision vector and the reverse entailment label. This is substantive verification but not formal entailment, and correlated model errors remain possible. There is no accuracy guarantee. Prompt-injection instructions are explicitly treated as data; this instruction alone cannot guarantee immunity.

Exact decision-vector matching can reduce liveness when models disagree. No fallback model output, automatic retry, appeal or privileged override exists in the contract. The network itself may rotate leaders during its normal consensus lifecycle; that is distinct from a client resending a transaction. A finalized REJECTED result is a successful assessment transaction, not a network failure.

READY means at least one attempt passed, not that all later attempts passed. Consumers must check state PUBLISHED and use exactly `attempts[published]`, the bound source hash, candidate hash and protocol. A record ID is owner-scoped. No global attestation, document signature, external enforcement, fee distribution or fund transfer is provided.

Stable Studionet is a hosted development network. Its operator and validator/provider configuration remain infrastructure assumptions. Recorded validator votes are reported as observed; configured participant counts do not establish independent operators or production decentralization.
